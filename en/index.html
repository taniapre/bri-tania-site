About me

I was born in Val Seriana, but part of my roots are in Plymouth, in South West England.

Growing up surrounded by two languages made me fall in love with everything related to language. From an early age I knew that everything had at least two names, and that gave me a particular perspective on life.

Communication is my daily bread: after working for 10 years in corporate communications, I chose to follow my own path, offering a range of services in English.

My specialty is ideas, and my passion is creating engaging content and proposing activities that entertain people while helping them reach their personal or professional goals.

BriTania comes from the idea of combining the fortune of growing up in a bilingual family with my professional experience in communications.

With this project I want to put my knowledge of English at the service of people and of the wonderful area where I was born and raised.

Services

Services

Courses.

Courses and lessons for every level and every age, one-to-one or in groups, to learn, strengthen or keep your English. How? Through engaging, always-varied activities built around real interaction and real communication, with the aim of supporting you in reaching your goals.

  • Native-level expert with international TEFL qualification
  • 100% personalised pathways
  • Teaching focused on your goals
  • A fun, effective and practical method

Services

Events.

Language events for people who already have a good level of English but few chances to practise it. BriTania events are informal occasions designed so you can converse in English and enjoy social life at the same time.

You might find what suits you among the BriTania formats. To stay updated on upcoming events, visit my social pages.

Do you run a venue?

If you run a venue yourself and would like to offer this opportunity to your customers, I would be happy to design your new, engaging language event together with you.

Services

Copywriting and translations

Many companies and organisations need to reach an international audience. Yet a simple translation is often not enough to bring out the best in your pay-off, website, brochure, video or social posts. That is why, alongside translation, I also offer copywriting: writing original English content to ensure a more natural, effective and engaging message.

  • English content creation for companies, institutions and organisations
  • Translations from Italian to English and from English to Italian

Formats

English Tasting

Themed tastings that let you discover food and drink from English-speaking countries (and beyond) while chatting with people who, like you, want to keep their English in shape and find chances to connect and socialise. (min. 8 participants)

Travel & Songs

The first situational course to pick up the English you need for travel. A full immersion packed with activities and songs, taking you through the key stages of a journey toward an ideal destination and getting you ready for your next trip abroad. (min. 4 participants)

English Breakfast

The course built on English and biscuits (and more) at breakfast time. Designed for small groups of friends and acquaintances who want to learn English in a familiar, relaxed setting, at their favourite café or venue. Ideal if you have flexible hours or free time in the morning. (min. 4 participants)

TV & Book Club

Themed meet-ups to discuss in English the plots and everything you loved (or hated) about a particular book, film or TV series. The club also offers guided viewing or reading of stories in the original language, so you can enjoy all those details lost in dubbing or translation. (min. 8 participants)

Back on Track

A summer campus for middle and high school students who need to face a September resit or strengthen their English before the new school year. Unlike classic one-to-one tutoring, Back on Track offers engaging activities and peer exchange in small groups of 2–3 students of the same age and level. (min. 2 participants)
