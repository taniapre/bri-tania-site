Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. They can be technical (needed for the site to work) or used for profiling/marketing.

2. What this site uses

BriTania does not install profiling, analytics or advertising cookies.

  • Technical / session data that the browser or hosting infrastructure (GitHub Pages) may use to deliver pages correctly: these are necessary and do not require consent under Italian Garante cookie guidance and ePrivacy rules.
  • Fonts: typefaces are self-hosted; they are not loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Contact form: submission goes to FormSubmit only after explicit consent; it is not a tracking cookie on our domain.

3. Banner

A cookie consent banner is not required for purely technical processing. For personal data processing (form, hosting, email), see the Privacy Policy.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may affect browsing.
